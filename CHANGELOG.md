# Changelog

## 1.0.0: First Bloom

- Rewritten from scratch as **Spore & Stem** for Minecraft 1.21.1 on Fabric and NeoForge (formerly Genetically
  Modified Monsters; nothing from it is kept). See [patchnotes/1.0.0.md](patchnotes/1.0.0.md).
- Three giant fungi with full wood sets, nine 3D small mushrooms, fungal blocks, cultivation, ten foods, the Mycosis
  infection, seven creatures and the Myceliarch boss.
- Older Minecraft versions are dropped for now and will be ported from the 1.21.1 codebase.

## Genetically Modified Monsters 1.0.1

- Added Minecraft 26.3 support; published each loader/game jar separately.

## Genetically Modified Monsters 1.0.0
