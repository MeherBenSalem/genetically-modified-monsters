## Grow something strange

**Spore & Stem** fills your world with fungi. Giant **Glowcaps** hang curtains of light over the Mushroom Fields and the
caves, springy **Bouncecaps** crowd the meadows and shaggy **Inkcaps** drip ink in the swamps. Each one gives a full
building wood. Nine small mushrooms, all modelled in real 3D, go into a forager's kitchen; cultivation logs and
Mycelial Soil let you farm them. **Mycosis**, a slow fungal infection, makes the dead rise as Shamblers. Seven
creatures live among the caps, from tameable Sporelings to the Mimicap that only looks like a toadstool. Under it all
stirs the **Myceliarch**.

Every texture, 3D model, creature animation and sound was made for this mod. It needs nothing beyond Fabric API on
Fabric. There are no new biomes: everything grows into the vanilla world through tags a datapack can change.

![The Giants](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/header-giants.png)

![Fungi the size of trees](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/card-giants.png)

- **Huge Glowcap:** a slender stem under a flat, glowing parasol. Its gills hang in curtains of light (light 14) and
  let luminous spores drift down. Mushroom Fields, and cave floors deep underground.
- **Huge Bouncecap:** a stout stem and a puffy pink dome. Land on the cap and you bounce, with no fall damage.
  Flower forests, meadows and the Mushroom Fields.
- **Huge Inkcap:** a tall stem inside a shaggy bell that drips ink. Dark forests, swamps and the Mushroom Fields.
- **Bone meal** grows each one from its small mushroom on dirt, moss, mycelium or Mycelial Soil, given the room.

![Fungal Woods](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/header-woods.png)

![Three fungal woods](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/card-woods.png)

Each giant gives a full wood: **stem, stripped stem, hyphae, stripped hyphae, planks, stairs, slab, fence, fence gate,
door, trapdoor, button and pressure plate**, plus its cap block. Glowcap wood is cool blue-grey with teal grain,
Bouncecap wood a rosy tan, Inkcap wood a deep charcoal violet. The doors and trapdoors carry each species' motif.

- An axe strips the stems. Every recipe that takes any planks or logs works with them: crafting tables, chests,
  sticks, tools, barrels and more.
- Like nether stems, fungal wood never catches fire, and it still burns in a furnace.
- **Glowcap Lantern** (light 15): standing or hanging, from glowcap stems, Lumen Spores and Mycelial Thread.

![Nine Mushrooms](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/header-fungi.png)

![Every one does something](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/card-fungi.png)

| Mushroom | Eaten raw | Found |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Glowcap** | Night Vision 10 s (and it glows, light 9) | Mushroom Fields, dark forests, caves |
| **Bouncecap** | Jump Boost 10 s | Flower forests, meadows, birch forests |
| **Inkcap** | not edible; crafts black dye | Swamps, dark forests, old growth taiga |
| **Morel** | 2 hunger; roast it for 6 | Forests and birch forests |
| **Chanterelle** | 2 hunger | Taiga and birch forests |
| **Deathcap** | Poison II and Mycosis. Don't. | Dark forests |
| **Frostcap** | Fire Resistance 10 s, a little Slowness | Snowy biomes |
| **Emberstool** | Haste 10 s (it smoulders, light 7) | Deep caves, crimson forest, basalt deltas |
| **Puffball** | 2 hunger; nine make a Giant Puffball | Plains, meadows, savanna |

- Placed, every one is a real 3D model, randomly turned and offset like flowers. All nine go in flower pots.
- They live on dirt, moss, mycelium and Mycelial Soil in any light, and on any solid block in the dark. Bone meal
  spreads the small ones to a free spot nearby.

![Into the Undergrowth](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/header-world.png)

![Life on every surface](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/card-world.png)

- **Mycelial Veils** hang from cave ceilings and grow down like vines. Some carry glowing bulbs (light 10): use one
  to pick Lumen Spores. Shears or a Foraging Knife cut Mycelial Thread from them. You can climb them.
- **Shelf Fungus:** turkey-tail brackets on tree trunks, one to three shelves that grow over time. They are solid:
  climb them like steps.
- **Glowcap Gills** hang under every giant Glowcap and drop Lumen Spores.
- **Giant Puffballs:** land on one and it puffs a cloud of spores and halves your fall damage.
- **Fairy Rings** of mushrooms in plains and forests (one in 48 chunks), **Truffle Soil** under the forests.

![Grow Your Own](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/header-cultivation.png)

![Cultivation logs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/card-cultivation.png)

- **Cultivation Log** (3 logs over Mycelial Thread, Mycelial Soil and Mycelial Thread, makes 2): use any of Glowcap,
  Bouncecap, Inkcap, Morel, Chanterelle, Frostcap or Emberstool on a fresh log to inoculate it. It fruits through
  four stages, twice as fast on Mycelial Soil and slower under an open daytime sky. Use a ripe log for 2-3
  mushrooms; it falls back to its first flush and fruits again.
- **Mycelial Soil** (dirt, bone meal and any 2 mushrooms, makes 2): every mushroom, vanilla ones too, lives on it in
  any light, and it slowly spreads them or grows the giants.
- **Foraging Knife** (iron, stick and Mycelial Thread): mines fungal blocks fast, cuts a better share of spores, gel
  and ink from caps, and takes Mycelial Thread from veils.

![Mycosis](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/header-mycosis.png)

![The infection](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/card-mycosis.png)

- **Mycosis** comes from Shambler hits, Deathcaps, spore clouds, Spore Globs and Spore Bombs. It deals a point of
  damage every 4 seconds (twice as often per level) and never takes you below one heart.
- **The infected dead rise.** A zombie, husk, drowned, villager, pillager, vindicator, witch or player that dies with
  Mycosis claws back up out of the ground as a Shambler.
- **Cures:** milk, Forager's Stew and the Antifungal Tonic. **Spore Ward** (the tonic, a full set of Mycoleather or
  the Crown of the Bloom) makes you immune.
- **Spore Bomb** (Puffball, Deathcap and Mycelial Thread, makes 2): throw it for a cloud of Mycosis and Slowness.
- Fungal creatures shrug it all off.

![Creatures](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/header-creatures.png)

![Friends in the dark](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/card-friends.png)

- **Sporeling:** a waddling little mushroom in one of four caps (Glowcap, Bouncecap, Inkcap or Chanterelle) by
  biome. It tends the fungi around it. Tame it with Truffles (one in three); tamed, it follows, sits when you use it
  and sheds a mushroom of its kind every 5-10 minutes. Breed them with Truffles.
- **Truffler:** a mossy boar with a mushroom garden on its back. It sniffs the ground and now and then digs up a
  Truffle, and it roots out Truffle Soil nearby. Breed them with Truffles.
- **Glowmoth:** soft glowing wings, drawn to light and to Glowcaps. Drops Lumen Spores.

![Fungal foes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/card-foes.png)

| Creature | Health | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bloatshroom** | 16 | Waddles close, swells and bursts into Mycosis, Nausea and Blindness. Breaks no blocks. Fire sets it off |
| **Shambler** | 26 | Cap-headed walker whose hits infect. It doesn't burn, but it wilts in full sunlight |
| **Sporemaw** | 20 | Waits burrowed with only its cap showing, then rises and lobs arcing Spore Globs. Hit it three times and it burrows away |
| **Mimicap** | 22 | Sits as a plain red or brown toadstool. Come within five blocks and it unfolds six legs, climbs walls and bites (Rooted) |

![The Myceliarch](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/header-boss.png)

![The Myceliarch](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/card-boss.png)

![Call it up](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/card-summon.png)

![The Myceliarch rises](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/gifs/myceliarch-rises.gif)

**Summon it** with a **Fruiting Heart** (4 Lumen Spores, 2 Truffles, 2 Mycoleather and a Mycelial Core from Shamblers,
Mimicaps or Sporemaws). Use it on Mycelium, Mycelial Soil, Podzol or Moss: roots and spores rise for three seconds,
then the Myceliarch climbs out of the earth with its boss bar. Only one at a time within 96 blocks.

![Root and spore](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/card-fight.png)

- **Slam** up close: 14 damage in front of it and a ring of Root Spikes. A ring of spores on the ground shows you
  where it will land.
- **Root Eruption** at range: the ground cracks in a line, then twelve Root Spikes burst through, 8 damage each and
  Rooted.
- **Spore Nova:** its cap swells as spores are drawn in, then a ring of Mycosis II bursts out seven blocks and shoves
  everything back.
- **Brood Call:** two Bloatshrooms and a Shambler.
- **Second bloom** at half health: it roars, its gills burn brighter, it moves faster and attacks more often, and it
  adds **Spore Rain**, globs that fall around you (each landing spot is marked first).
- 300 health and 10 armor. It never despawns, can't be pushed, breaks no blocks and stays within 40 blocks of where
  it rose. Every attack is telegraphed; a Slam takes about half the health of a player in full iron on Normal.

![Spoils](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/header-spoils.png)

![Bloomreaver and Crown](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/card-spoils.png)

Felled, the Myceliarch collapses into spores and drops the **Spore Heart**, 4-8 Mycoleather, 8-16 Lumen Spores and
250 experience.

- **Bloomreaver** (Spore Heart, a Diamond Sword, 2 Lumen Spores and 2 Mycoleather): 8 attack damage, and every hit
  infects. **Use** it for a **Spore Burst**: Mycosis and Slowness on everything around you, and a shove (10 s
  cooldown).
- **Crown of the Bloom** (Spore Heart, a Golden Helmet and 2 Glowcap Caps): Night Vision and Spore Ward while you wear
  it, and Sporelings trust you.
- **Mycoleather** (3 mushroom caps and a Mycelial Thread, makes 2): armor between leather and iron, and a full set
  gives Spore Ward.

![Configuration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/header-config.png)

![Tune the bloom](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/card-config.png)

`config/spore_and_stem.json` is created on first launch, with a comment beside every key. Missing keys are added and
your values are kept.

- `mycosis_enabled`, `mycosis_rising` and `mycosis_damage_interval`: turn the infection off, keep the dead from
  rising, or slow it down.
- `myceliarch_health` and `myceliarch_damage_multiplier`.
- `sporeling_shed_min` and `sporeling_shed_max` (minutes), `truffler_dig_chance`.

![Everything Else](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/header-everything.png)

- **15 advancements**, from *Lights Out, Caps On* to *Uprooted* and *Reap What You Sow*.
- **55 sounds**, all with subtitles, from the Sporeling's squeak to the Myceliarch's roar.
- **Spawn eggs** for all eight creatures, and everything in its own **Spore & Stem** creative tab.
- **Datapack friendly:** every feature and every spawn is placed through biome tags under
  `data/spore_and_stem/tags/worldgen/biome/`, and the infected dead are the `spore_and_stem:mycosis_risers` entity
  tag. Mushrooms are in `c:mushrooms`, Mycoleather in `c:leathers`, Bouncecap Gel in `c:slime_balls` (on 1.20.1 also
  `forge:mushrooms`, `forge:leather` and `forge:slimeballs`).
- **No new biomes, no world-type changes:** it works in existing worlds; new chunks grow the fungi.
- Mushrooms, caps and puffballs go in the composter.

| Minecraft | Loaders | Java | Also install |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.21.1 | Fabric, NeoForge | 21+ | Fabric API (Fabric only) |
| 1.20.1 | Fabric, Forge | 17+ | Fabric API (Fabric only) |

- Download the file with your loader and Minecraft version in its name, for example
  `spore_and_stem-forge-1.20.1-2.0.0.jar`. The content is the same on both Minecraft versions.
- Install Spore & Stem on **both the server and every client**, at the same version.
- Spore & Stem is a mod. It does not work as a Paper, Spigot or Folia plugin.
- Coming from *Genetically Modified Monsters* (1.x)? Spore & Stem replaces it with a new mod id: its creepers and
  spawn eggs disappear from old worlds.

![Questions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/genetically-modified-monsters/main/marketing/images/header-faq.png)

**Do I need a new world?**
No. Fungi, creatures and features grow in chunks generated after you install it. Explore somewhere new, or grow the
giants yourself with bone meal.

**Where do I find the giant mushrooms?**
Head for a Mushroom Fields island: it fills with all three. Glowcaps also grow on cave floors deep underground,
Bouncecaps in flower forests and meadows, Inkcaps in dark forests and swamps.

**Mycosis keeps finding me.**
Drink milk, eat a Forager's Stew or drink an Antifungal Tonic. For the long run, wear a full set of Mycoleather: it
gives Spore Ward. Or turn Mycosis off in the config.

**Will the Bloatshroom blow up my base?**
No. It bursts into spores, not an explosion, and breaks no blocks. No creature in this mod breaks your blocks, the
Myceliarch included.

**How do I get a Mycelial Core for the Fruiting Heart?**
Shamblers drop one 5% of the time, Mimicaps 8% and Sporemaws 4%, more with Looting.
