# Changelog

## 2.0.0: First Bloom

- Rewritten from scratch as **Spore & Stem** for Minecraft 1.21.1 (Fabric, NeoForge) and 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge),
  formerly Genetically Modified Monsters; nothing from it is kept, including the mod id. See
  [patchnotes/2.0.0.md](patchnotes/2.0.0.md).
- Three giant fungi with full wood sets, nine 3D small mushrooms, fungal blocks, cultivation, ten foods, the Mycosis
  infection, seven creatures and the Myceliarch boss.
- Other Minecraft versions are dropped for now and will be ported from the same codebase.

## Genetically Modified Monsters 1.0.1

- Added Minecraft 26.3 support; published each loader/game jar separately.

## Genetically Modified Monsters 1.0.0

- Initial release: seven red genetically modified Creeper variants.
