# Spore & Stem 2.0.1

## Fixed

- **Server crash from a Truffler (Ticking entity).** When a Truffler finished rooting out Truffle Soil, it could tick
  its digging once more after the soil was gone and crash the server with `NullPointerException: "this.soil" is null`
  in `TrufflerEntity$RootOutGoal.tick`. A world with that Truffler near a player couldn't load. Fixed on every
  version; worlds that hit the crash load again after updating.

No other gameplay, config or save changes.
